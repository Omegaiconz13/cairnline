FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. Find quick answers to common questions about PipNex.

What is PipNex?

PipNex is an AI trading platform for Forex: chart analysis, market structure detection, signal generation and MT5 automation in one workspace.

How does the AI chart analysis work?

Our AI reads the chart in front of it — structure, order blocks and liquidity — then returns a confidence-scored trade plan with entry, stop-loss and target.

What markets does PipNex support?

Forex pairs, plus the majors, minors and metals available through your MT5 broker.

Is there a free trial available?

You can create an account and explore the dashboard before subscribing. Plans are month-to-month with no lock-in.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes. Cancelling stops future renewals and keeps access until the end of the current period.

What payment methods do you accept?

Card and the major online payment methods available at checkout.

How accurate is the AI analysis?

Signals are confidence-scored and the reasoning is shown, so you can judge each setup instead of trusting a number.

Do you offer refunds?

Refunds are available only within 24 hours of purchase and only if you have not used any platform features after payment, plus cases of duplicate charges or failed activation. Cancelling your subscription stops future renewals but does not refund the current period.
